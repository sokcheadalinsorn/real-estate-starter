More than ten years of extensive experience in property brokerage, valuation, management, development, and advisory are all brought to the table by Tim raw. Many high-value transactions have been successfully closed as a result of his leadership. Tim raw graduated with a Bachelor of English Literature from the Institute of Foreign Languages and a Bachelor of Business Management from the University of Cambodia.
